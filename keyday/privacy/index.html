Who We Are

Tadbir Labs ("we", "us", "our") develops Keyday, an Android app for property inspection and handover reports. We are based in Egypt.

This policy applies to the Keyday app for Android. It explains what the app keeps on your phone, what can leave your phone and how, and the choices you have.

Information Stored on Your Phone

Everything you enter in Keyday is saved in the app's private storage on your phone, mostly in a local database. Photos are saved as files. Other apps cannot read this storage.

  • Projects: the title, client, address or unit, inspection date and inspector name you enter, and the project's areas.
  • Issues: titles, areas, trades, priority, status, responsible party, due date, location detail and notes.
  • Photos: the photos you take or pick for an issue, their captions, roles and mark-up, and smaller copies used as thumbnails.
  • Company details and sign-offs: the company name, logo and contact details you add for your reports, and the sign-off details you enter on a report.
  • Phrases and settings: your own defect phrases, the language and report options you choose, and the last place you were in the app so it can reopen there.

Photos taken with a phone camera can contain hidden details (EXIF data), which can include the location where the photo was taken. Keyday keeps your original photos unchanged, so the copies inside the app keep these details. Keyday itself never reads your location.

Keyday never sends your data to us. The app has no internet permission, and we run no server for your data. We only see a report or file if you choose to send it to us.

Information That Leaves Your Phone

Files You Share

When you share a PDF report, a CSV export with photos, or a project archive, the file is made on your phone and goes only where you choose to send or save it, through Android's share menu. From then on, that destination's own privacy policy applies.

  • PDF reports and CSV exports: photos in them have their hidden EXIF details removed, including any location.
  • Project archives: an archive is a full backup of a project, so it keeps your original photos with their hidden details. Keyday reminds you of this when you export one.

Keep these files private: reports, exports and archives contain your project details and are not encrypted by Keyday.

Purchases

You buy Keyday on Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy, and gives us the order records it gives every developer, such as the order number, product, price, date and country or region, for refunds, tax and accounting. We never receive your payment card details. If you redeem a promo code, Google handles that the same way.

Crash Reports and Statistics from Google

If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics data, Google may give us crash reports and statistics about Keyday in Google Play Console, such as crash logs, phone model, Android version and app version. These reports describe crashes and app performance; they do not include your projects or photos. You can turn this sharing off in your phone's settings.

Device Backups

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Keyday, because it would restore the database without the photos. Your phone's cloud backup therefore does not include Keyday's data. To keep a copy, export a project archive and store it where you choose.

When you move to a new phone with a cable or a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android may copy Keyday's data straight to the new phone. This goes only between your two phones; we receive nothing.

When You Contact Us

If you email us or message us on Telegram, we receive your email address or Telegram account and whatever you include, such as screenshots or files. We use it only to answer you.

Keyday does not include advertising networks, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, social media SDKs, or location services. We do not sell your information, and we do not use or share it for advertising.

App Permissions

  • Camera: Keyday opens your phone's own camera app to take a photo, so it does not ask for the camera permission.
  • Photos: to add photos from your gallery, Keyday uses Android's system photo picker (or file picker on phones without one), which gives it only the photos you choose. It does not ask for access to your storage or media.

Keyday does not ask for internet access, contacts, microphone, location or phone calls.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleted projects, issues and photos wait 30 days in Keyday's Trash, where you can restore them. After that, Keyday removes them the next time you open it.
  • To remove everything, uninstall Keyday, or clear the app's storage in Android's settings. This removes the database, photos and settings.
  • Reports, exports and archives you shared or saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.
  • Google keeps order records according to its own policies and legal duties, such as tax law.
  • We keep your emails and messages only as long as we need them to help you and to keep basic records.

Security

  • Your data is protected by your phone's security: each app's data is kept separate, and modern phones encrypt their storage, protected by your screen lock. Keyday does not add its own encryption to the database or to exported files.
  • Because Keyday has no internet access, your data cannot be sent from the app over a network.

Your part: use a screen lock, keep Android updated, and keep shared reports and archives private.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it is used.

  • Data on your phone: you can view, change, export and delete it in the app at any time. We hold no copy.
  • Order records held by Google, and messages you sent us: email us and we will help. We may ask for an order number to find the right record.
  • No sale or targeted advertising: we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as defined by California law (CCPA/CPRA). We do not make automated decisions about you.
  • EU, EEA and UK users: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email support@tadbirlabs.net. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Children's Privacy

Keyday is a work tool for property inspections and is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when the app, the services it uses, or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we may also mention them in the app's release notes on Google Play.
