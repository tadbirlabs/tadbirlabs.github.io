Subscriptions, Payments & Refunds

Free Features

You can use Tadbir for free, with no subscription, including:

  • Unlimited transactions, categories and tags

Some features have free limits: 2 accounts, 1 budget, 3 recurring transactions, 1 savings goal and 1 debt.

Tadbir Premium is an optional, auto-renewing in-app subscription. It removes the free limits above and adds:
