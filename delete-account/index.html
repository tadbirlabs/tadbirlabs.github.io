Delete Account

Last updated: Oct 9, 2025

Tadbir works offline and does not create server accounts by default. If we introduce optional cloud accounts in the future, this page will describe the steps to delete your account and associated data with the relevant provider.

If you are using device-local data only, you can delete your data from within the app or by uninstalling the app.
