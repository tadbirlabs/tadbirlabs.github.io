Privacy PolicyOffline-first

Last updated: Oct 9, 2025

Who we are

TadbirLabs (“we”, “us”) develops Tadbir, an Arabic-first, offline-first expense tracker. Contact: tadbirlabs@gmail.com.

Scope

This policy explains how your information is handled when you use Tadbir mobile apps and related pages at tadbirlabs.github.io.

Data model (offline-first)

By default, your entries, categories, budgets and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal data to our servers because we operate no backend. Any optional cloud features you enable (e.g., backups) will clearly state the provider and terms before activation.

Information we process

  • On-device data (local): transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, preferences.
  • Optional cloud sync/backup (if enabled): your data may be transferred to the chosen provider (e.g., Google Drive/iCloud). In such cases, the provider’s privacy policy applies in addition to ours.
  • Diagnostics & analytics: By default, we do not collect analytics. If we later add privacy-respecting analytics (e.g., anonymous, aggregate), we will disclose it here and inside the app, and provide an opt-out.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save and restore your data locally.
  • Notifications (optional): to remind you about entries or budgets.

Data retention & deletion

All local data remains on your device until you delete it via in-app controls or uninstall the app. If you enable an online account or cloud backup in the future, we will provide a public Delete Account page describing how to request deletion with that provider, where applicable.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we’ll assist with deletion steps.

Security

Local storage reduces network exposure. Nevertheless, protect your device with a passcode/biometrics. If we integrate third-party storage, transport will use HTTPS; at-rest encryption depends on the provider. We recommend enabling your device’s encrypted backups.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Since we do not operate a user database by default, most actions can be performed within the app locally.

Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll update the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, announce changes in-app or on this page.
