Our Privacy Commitment

At TadbirLabs, we believe financial privacy is a fundamental right. That's why Tadbir is built from the ground up as an offline-first application:

  • No server-side storage: We don't maintain databases with your financial transactions
  • No behavioral tracking: We don't track which features you use or how often you use them
  • No advertising: We never show ads, so we have no incentive to collect your data
  • No data sales: We will never, ever sell your personal or financial information
  • Optional cloud features: All online features require explicit opt-in consent
  • Transparent policies: Our privacy policy explains exactly what happens to your data (which is: almost nothing, because it stays on your device)
