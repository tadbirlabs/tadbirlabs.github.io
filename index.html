Questions

Common questions about Tadbir

What is Tadbir?

Tadbir is a personal finance app made by Tadbir Labs. You record your spending and income, set budgets, track debts and save toward goals, all in one place. It works without internet.

Is Tadbir free?

The essentials are free: as many transactions as you like, 2 accounts, 1 budget, 1 savings goal, 1 active debt and 3 repeating transactions, with backup, restore and export. Premium, monthly or yearly, lifts these limits and adds receipt photos, App Lock and CSV import. New subscribers can try Premium free for 7 days when the store offers the trial; the app shows the offer and the price.

Does Tadbir work without internet?

Yes. Tadbir works offline: recording transactions, budgets, debts, goals and charts. Subscribing to Premium or restoring it needs a connection, and files you export or share go wherever you send them.

Does Tadbir read my bank messages?

No. Tadbir does not ask for access to your messages or to notifications from banking apps, and it does not connect to your bank account. You enter your transactions yourself. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat, as the privacy policy explains.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and there are no ads. Open the app and start.

Is Tadbir available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It is free to download from the App Store and Google Play. Each store page shows which system versions it supports.

Which currency does Tadbir use?

The one you choose. You can also keep accounts in different currencies, with exchange rates you set.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, export a full backup file from the app and save it where the new phone can open it. On the new phone, install Tadbir and restore that file; restoring replaces the Tadbir data already on that phone. Backup files contain your financial data and are not encrypted, so keep them private. The exact steps are on the support page.
